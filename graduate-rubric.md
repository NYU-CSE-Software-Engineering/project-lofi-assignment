## Grading Rubric

| **Category** | **Excellent (90-100%)** | **Satisfactory (70-89%)** | **Needs Improvement (<70%)** | **Score** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Low-Fidelity Wireframe Quality** | All three of the most critical screens are present and together cover the core functional requirements (Section 2.0 of the specification). UI placement, navigation elements, and content priority are clear, and the "low-fidelity" nature is maintained (focus on layout/UX over art). | Wireframes cover most core functions but miss 1-2 secondary features, or one of the three screens is weak. The layout is somewhat cluttered or confusing. | Wireframes are incomplete, missing core screens, or are too high-fidelity (distracting from the UX). | __/80 |
| **Team Contributions Page** | A dedicated page clearly lists every member and provides a detailed breakdown of their specific tasks and artifacts. | Contribution page is present, but descriptions are vague (e.g., "*did the sketches*") without detail. | Contribution page is missing or does not account for all registered team members. | __/10 |
| **Deliverable Format** | All deliverables are submitted as a single PDF file through Brightspace. Wireframes are embedded correctly and are high-resolution scans of hand-drawn images. The contributions page is the final page. | Submitted PDF is missing pages or sections of the assignment, or scans are hard to read. | Submitted file is missing, in the wrong format, not submitted through Brightspace, or otherwise significantly problematic. | __/10 |
| **Total** | | | | __ /100 |

**Automatic grade of 0:**

- Any team member who does not participate in the deliverable receives a 0 for the assignment, even though it is a team assignment.
- Wireframes created with a drawing or diagramming tool instead of by hand invalidate the submission, and the team receives a 0.
